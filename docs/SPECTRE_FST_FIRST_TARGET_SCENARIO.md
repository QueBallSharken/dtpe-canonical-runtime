## 2. SCENARIO ID

`fst_first_target_scenario_001`

## 3. SCENARIO NAME

Boundary continuity claim with incomplete system-wide refusal continuity evidence.

---

## 4. SCENARIO STATEMENT

A system claims that the same governing basis remained live across a mutation-relevant path.

The local refusal boundary appears to exist and be real.

However, the system-wide refusal continuity is not strongly demonstrated under in-flight authority or delegation change.

The scenario is bounded to that continuity question only.

---

## 5. TARGETED STRESS CATEGORY

This scenario targets:

- boundary continuity stress

It may later be cross-tested against:
- authority continuity stress
- proof continuity stress

But the first bounded target is boundary continuity stress only.

---

## 6. CLAIM UNDER TEST

The bounded claim under test is:

A continuity claim can remain supportable only if refusal continuity is not merely local, but remains supportable across the claimed system path strongly enough for the stated scope.

---

## 7. EXPECTED RESULT DISCIPLINE

Safe expected outcomes for this scenario include:

- PARTIAL
- UNVERIFIABLE
- CONTRADICTION_EXPOSED

A STRONG result should not survive unless the scenario evidence is tightened beyond the current bounded setup.
